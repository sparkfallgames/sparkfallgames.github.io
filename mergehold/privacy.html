护主远征 Mergehold – 隐私政策

最后更新 / Last updated: 2026-07-24

你的数据只存在你的设备上

护主远征为隐私而设计。你的对局进度、军衔天梯、图鉴收藏、战绩复盘、称号头像与全部偏好设置都只保存在你的设备本地。我们不运营任何服务器，不收集、不传输、也无法访问你的任何数据。游戏不需要注册账号。

单机完全离线

不联网也能玩全部单机内容：对手由设备本地的 AI 扮演（与你同规则、同数值真实执行）；每日地图轮换由设备上的日期直接计算得出。单机游玩从不向互联网发送任何内容。

联机对战（可选，由 Apple Game Center 提供）

真人匹配与好友对战是可选功能，由 Apple 的系统服务 Game Center 提供（与 iCloud、App 内购同级）。启用后，对局操作指令等结构化游戏数据经 Apple 的基础设施在双方设备之间中转——我们不运营任何服务器，不接收、不存储对局之外的任何数据，也无法访问你的 Game Center 账号信息。游戏内没有聊天或任何自由文本传输。不登录 Game Center 不影响单机游玩。Game Center 的隐私实践见 Apple 隐私政策。

无广告、无追踪

护主远征不含任何第三方广告、统计分析 SDK 或任何形式的追踪。

内购说明

护主 VIP（月度/年度自动续订订阅）与主公皮肤（一次性买断，纯外观）均由 Apple 全程处理交易。我们不接收、不存储任何支付信息。订阅可随时在系统设置中取消。

你的权利（GDPR / CCPA）

由于数据只存在于你的设备上：访问、更正数据可直接在游戏内完成；删除数据 = 在游戏内清除或直接删除 App。我们无法访问你的任何数据，因此也不存在可出售的个人信息——我们不出售任何个人信息。隐私相关问询请联系下方邮箱。

儿童隐私

护主远征不收集任何人（包括儿童）的个人信息。没有自建账号、没有聊天功能；联机只传输对局操作等结构化游戏数据，且经由 Apple Game Center 中转。

政策变更

本政策如有更新会发布在本页面，并刷新顶部日期。

Mergehold – Privacy Policy (English)

Your data stays on your device

Mergehold is designed to be private. Your match progress, ladder rank, codex collection, match history, cosmetics and preferences are stored locally on your device. We do not operate any servers, and we never collect, transmit, or have access to your data. Mergehold does not require an account.

Fully offline single-player

Everything single-player works without a connection: opponents are played by an on-device AI that executes the same rules and numbers as you; the daily map rotation is computed from the calendar date on your device. Single-player never sends anything to the internet.

Online play (optional, powered by Apple Game Center)

Real-player matchmaking and friend matches are optional features powered by Game Center, Apple's system service. When enabled, structured game data (match commands) is relayed between the two devices through Apple's infrastructure — we operate no servers, receive and store nothing beyond the match itself, and cannot access your Game Center account. There is no chat or free-text transmission of any kind. You can play all single-player content without signing in to Game Center. See Apple's Privacy Policy for Game Center's practices.

No ads, no tracking

Mergehold contains no third-party advertising, no analytics SDKs, and no tracking of any kind.

In-app purchases

Mergehold VIP (a monthly/yearly auto-renewing subscription) and one-time cosmetic lord skins are processed entirely by Apple. We do not receive or store any payment information. Subscriptions can be cancelled at any time in system settings.

Your rights (GDPR / CCPA)

Because your data lives only on your device: you can access and correct it in the app, and deleting your data means clearing it in the app or deleting the app itself. We cannot access your data, and we do not sell any personal information (there is none we could sell). For privacy inquiries, contact the email below.

Children's privacy

Mergehold does not collect personal information from anyone, including children. There are no custom accounts and no chat; online play only transmits structured game data, relayed by Apple Game Center.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.
