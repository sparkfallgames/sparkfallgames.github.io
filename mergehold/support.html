常见问题

哪些内容免费？VIP 有什么权益？

全部玩法（四种对局模式、44 位武将、天梯军衔、图鉴、每日免费抽）永久免费。护主 VIP 是自动续订订阅，权益为：出征不耗体力、每日抽 ×2、完整战绩复盘、专属外观。VIP 不出售任何战斗力，对局永远公平。另有两款一次性买断的主公皮肤，纯外观。

如何恢复购买？

在游戏内「设置」或 VIP 页面点「恢复购买」，使用当初购买时的同一 Apple 账户即可。

如何取消订阅？

打开系统「设置」→ 你的 Apple 账户 → 订阅，即可随时取消。取消后权益保留到当期结束。

对手是真人吗？

登录 Game Center 后，「匹配出征」会优先为你寻找真人对手（双方实时对战、同图同波同规则）；一时匹配不到人时，由设备本地的 AI 对手接替——同规则、同数值真实执行。你也可以邀请 Game Center 好友切磋（不耗体力、不计天梯星）。不登录 Game Center 则全部对局由 AI 扮演。

联机安全吗？我的数据会被上传吗？

联机由 Apple Game Center 提供，对局指令经 Apple 基础设施中转——我们不运营任何服务器，也不接触对局之外的任何数据。游戏内没有聊天功能。

我的进度存在哪里？

只存在你的设备本地。删除 App 会永久删除全部数据。

游戏需要联网吗？

单机不需要：四种模式、每日地图轮换等一切单机功能完全离线可玩。只有真人匹配与好友对战需要联网并登录 Game Center。

FAQ (English)

What is free and what does VIP include?

All gameplay (four battle modes, 44 generals, the ranked ladder, the codex and the free daily draw) is free forever. Mergehold VIP is an auto-renewing subscription: unlimited stamina, double daily draws, full match replays and exclusive cosmetics. VIP never sells combat power — matches are always fair. Two one-time cosmetic lord skins are also available.

How do I restore my purchase?

Tap "Restore Purchases" in Settings or on the VIP page, using the same Apple ID as the original purchase.

How do I cancel the subscription?

Open system Settings → your Apple account → Subscriptions. Benefits remain until the end of the current period.

Are opponents real players?

When signed in to Game Center, quick match looks for a real opponent first (live mirrored duel — same map, waves and rules). If no one is available right away, an on-device AI opponent steps in, executing the same rules and numbers. You can also invite Game Center friends for casual matches (no stamina cost, unranked). Without Game Center, all opponents are played by the AI.

Is online play private?

Online play is powered by Apple Game Center; match commands are relayed through Apple's infrastructure. We run no servers and never touch anything beyond the match itself. There is no chat.

Where is my progress stored?

Locally on your device only. Deleting the app permanently deletes its data.

Do I need a subscription?

VIP is an optional subscription for convenience and cosmetics only — no gameplay power is sold, and everything competitive is earnable by playing. Skins are one-time purchases.

Does it work offline?

Single-player modes run fully offline. Online matches need a connection through Apple Game Center; no account of ours is ever required.
