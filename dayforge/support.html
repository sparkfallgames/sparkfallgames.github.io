常见问题

哪些内容免费？Pro 有什么？

免费层可保留最多 3 个进行中的习惯，并支持每日打卡、基础连续天数与本周条。Pro 是一次买断 $6.99（不是订阅）：不限习惯数、年热力网格、洞察摘要、CSV 导出、主屏幕与锁屏小组件、自定义提醒时刻，以及习惯色/图标。全程无广告、无追踪。

如何恢复购买？

打开 App 内「设置」或付费页，点「恢复购买」，使用当初购买时的同一 Apple 账户即可。

我的习惯数据存在哪里？

只存在你的设备本地（含 App Group 中的小组件快照）。删除 App 会永久删除全部数据。我们不运营服务器，也无法访问你的习惯记录。

需要联网吗？

日常打卡与查看连续天数不需要联网。仅在购买或恢复 Pro 时需要网络（由 Apple StoreKit 处理）。通知均为本机调度，不需要我们的服务器。

通知会追踪我吗？

不会。习惯提醒、连续天数风险提醒与周日报均为可选的本机通知，默认关闭或需你主动开启。我们不发远程推送，也不读取你的通知记录做分析。可在 DayForge 设置或 iOS 系统设置中随时关闭。

小组件不更新怎么办？

先在 App 内勾选一项习惯并稍等几秒，让主 App 刷新 App Group 快照。若仍不更新，长按小组件选择「编辑小组件」后退出，让系统重新加载。小组件功能为 Pro 权益。

需要订阅吗？

不需要。免费层完整可用核心打卡，Pro 为一次性买断，无订阅、无试用倒计时、无假折扣。

FAQ (English)

What is free and what does Pro include?

The free tier supports up to 3 active habits, daily check-ins, basic streak counts, and the weekly strip. Pro is a one-time purchase of $6.99 (not a subscription): unlimited habits, the year heat grid, insight summaries, CSV export, Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets, custom reminder times, and habit colors/icons. No ads, no tracking, ever.

How do I restore my purchase?

Open Settings or the paywall in the app and tap “Restore Purchases”, using the same Apple ID as the original purchase.

Where is my habit data stored?

Locally on your device only (including the App Group snapshot used by widgets). Deleting the app permanently deletes all data. We do not operate servers and cannot access your habits.

Do I need a network connection?

Not for day-to-day check-ins and streaks. You only need the network when buying or restoring Pro (handled by Apple StoreKit). Notifications are scheduled on-device and do not use our servers.

Do notifications track me?

No. Habit reminders, streak-at-risk nudges, and the weekly summary are optional local notifications — off by default or until you enable them. We do not send remote push notifications or read your notification history for analytics. Turn categories off in DayForge Settings or revoke permission in iOS Settings anytime.

The widget isn’t updating

Check off a habit in the app and wait a few seconds so the main app refreshes the App Group snapshot. If it still looks stale, long-press the widget → Edit Widget and dismiss so iOS reloads it. Widgets are a Pro feature.

Do I need a subscription?

No. The free tier covers core check-ins, and Pro is a single one-time purchase — no subscription, no trial countdown, no fake discounts.
