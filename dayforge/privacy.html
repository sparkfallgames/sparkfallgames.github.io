DayForge – 隐私政策

最后更新 / Last updated: 2026-08-21

你的数据只存在你的设备上

DayForge 为隐私而设计。你的习惯、打卡记录、连续天数、提醒设置与偏好都以 JSON 形式保存在设备本地（含用于主屏幕与锁屏小组件的 App Group 快照）。我们不运营任何服务器，不收集、不传输、也无法访问你的习惯数据。App 不需要注册账号。

默认完全离线

创建习惯、勾选打卡、查看连续天数与本周条、阅读洞察摘要，均在设备上完成。正常使用下，DayForge 不会把你的习惯数据发给我们（我们也没有可接收的服务器）。

App Group（仅用于小组件）

DayForge 使用 Apple App Group，让主 App 与小组件扩展读取同一份本机习惯快照。数据留在 Apple 沙盒内，不会上传至 Sparkfall Games。

可选的本机通知

你可选择开启以下通知（均为本机调度，非远程推送）：

  • 习惯提醒 — 每个习惯自设时刻（需你主动开通知并设定时间）
  • 连续天数风险提醒 — 临近午夜尚未打卡时提醒（默认关）
  • 周日报 — 周日本地摘要（默认关）

我们不发营销推送，也不读取你的通知记录做分析。可在 DayForge 设置或 iOS 系统设置中随时关闭。

系统 API

DayForge 使用 UserDefaults 与文件时间戳保存设置并同步小组件快照（见 App 隐私清单中的 Required Reason API 声明）。这些数据仍留在设备上，不会因此上传到我们。

无广告、无追踪、无统计

DayForge 不含第三方广告、统计分析 SDK 或任何形式的追踪。这是永久产品政策。

内购说明

DayForge Pro 为一次性买断（非消耗型），由 Apple 通过 StoreKit 全程处理。我们不接收、不存储任何支付信息。购买状态由设备上的 StoreKit 校验；可随时在 App 内或 App Store 账户中恢复购买。

分享与导出

若你分享成就卡或使用 CSV 导出（Pro），内容由你自行选择发送渠道（信息、邮件、文件等）。我们不会收到被分享或导出的内容。

你的权利（GDPR / CCPA）

由于数据只存在于你的设备：访问与更正可在 App 内完成；删除可在 App 内清除习惯/数据或直接删除 App；Pro 用户可 CSV 导出备份。我们无法访问你的数据，也不出售任何个人信息。隐私相关问询请联系下方邮箱。

儿童隐私

DayForge 不收集任何人（包括儿童）的个人信息。没有账号，也没有社交功能。

政策变更

本政策如有更新会发布在本页面，并刷新顶部日期。

联系我们

如有任何疑问，请联系：support@sparkfallgames.com

DayForge – Privacy Policy (English)

Your data stays on your device

DayForge is designed to be private. Your habits, check-in history, streaks, reminder settings, and preferences are stored locally on your device as JSON files (including the App Group container used to mirror a snapshot for the Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets). We do not operate any servers, and we never collect, transmit, or have access to your habit data. DayForge does not require an account.

Fully offline by default

Creating habits, checking them off, viewing streaks, browsing the weekly strip, and reading insights all happen on your device. In normal use DayForge never sends your habit data to us (we do not run any servers that could receive it).

App Group (widget only)

DayForge uses an Apple App Group so the main app and widget extension can read the same on-device habit snapshot. This data stays in Apple's sandbox on your device and is not uploaded to Sparkfall Games.

Optional local notifications

You may optionally enable notifications:

  • Habit reminders — a time you choose per habit (off until you turn notifications on and set a time)
  • Streak at risk — a local nudge if you have not checked in near midnight (off by default)
  • Weekly summary — a local summary on Sunday (off by default)

These are scheduled on your device only. We do not send remote push notifications, do not operate a notification server, and do not read your notification history for analytics. You can turn categories off in DayForge Settings or revoke notification permission in iOS Settings at any time.

System APIs

DayForge uses UserDefaults and file timestamps to save settings and sync the widget snapshot (declared under Required Reason APIs in the app's privacy manifest). That data remains on your device and is not uploaded to us.

No ads, no tracking, no analytics

DayForge contains no third-party advertising, no analytics SDKs, and no tracking of any kind. We do not use your data for advertising or profiling. This is a permanent product policy.

In-app purchases

DayForge Pro is a one-time non-consumable purchase processed entirely by Apple through StoreKit. We do not receive or store any payment card or billing information. Your Pro entitlement is verified on-device by StoreKit; you can restore purchases anytime from the app or your App Store account.

Sharing and export

If you use the share sheet to send an achievement card, or export CSV (Pro), you choose where that file goes — Messages, Mail, Files, etc. We do not receive shared or exported content.

Your rights (GDPR / CCPA)

Because your data lives only on your device:

  • Access and correction — edit habits and history directly in the app
  • Deletion — delete individual habits, clear data in Settings, or delete the app to remove all local files
  • Portability — export CSV (Pro) for your own backup

We cannot access your habit data, and we do not sell personal information (there is none we could sell). For privacy inquiries, contact the email below.

Children's privacy

DayForge does not collect personal information from anyone, including children. There are no accounts and no social features.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date at the top.

Contact

If you have any questions about this policy, contact us at: support@sparkfallgames.com
