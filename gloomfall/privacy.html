Gloomfall – Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 17, 2026

Your data stays on your device

Gloomfall is designed to be private. Your roster, expedition progress, statistics and preferences are stored locally on your device. We do not operate any servers, and we never collect, transmit, or have access to your data. Gloomfall does not require an account.

The daily expedition works offline

The daily expedition is generated on your device from the calendar date itself, so every player in the world gets the same map without any network connection. Gloomfall never sends anything to the internet.

No ads, no tracking

This version of Gloomfall contains no third-party advertising, no analytics SDKs, and no tracking of any kind. If a future version introduces ads, this policy will be updated before release and any tracking will require your explicit consent through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt.

In-app purchases

Gloomfall: Full Saga is a one-time purchase processed entirely by Apple. We do not receive or store any payment information.

Children's privacy

Gloomfall does not collect personal information from anyone, including children. There are no accounts, no social features, and no data leaves the device.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.
