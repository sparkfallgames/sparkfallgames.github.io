PackNest – 隐私政策

最后更新 / Last updated: 2026-08-01

你的数据只存在你的设备上

PackNest 为隐私而设计。你的行程、装箱清单、勾选进度与偏好设置都只保存在你的设备本地（含用于小组件的 App Group 容器）。我们不运营任何服务器，不收集、不传输、也无法访问你的任何数据。App 不需要注册账号。

完全离线

创建行程、勾选物品、查看模板与导出 PDF 均在设备上完成。PackNest 从不向我们的服务器发送任何内容（我们也没有这类服务器）。

系统能力说明

PackNest 使用 UserDefaults 与本地文件时间戳等系统 API 保存设置与同步小组件快照（见 App 隐私清单中的 Required Reason API 声明）。这些数据仍留在设备上，不会因此上传到我们。

无广告、无追踪

PackNest 不含任何第三方广告、统计分析 SDK 或任何形式的追踪。这是永久产品政策。

内购说明

PackNest Pro 是一次性买断（非消耗型），由 Apple 全程处理交易。我们不接收、不存储任何支付信息。购买状态由设备上的 StoreKit 校验。

你的权利（GDPR / CCPA）

由于数据只存在于你的设备上：访问与更正可直接在 App 内完成；删除数据 = 在 App 内清除行程或直接删除 App。我们无法访问你的任何数据，因此也不存在可出售的个人信息——我们不出售任何个人信息。隐私相关问询请联系下方邮箱。

儿童隐私

PackNest 不收集任何人（包括儿童）的个人信息。没有账号，也没有社交功能。

政策变更

本政策如有更新会发布在本页面，并刷新顶部日期。

联系我们

PackNest – Privacy Policy (English)

Your data stays on your device

PackNest is designed to be private. Your trips, packing lists, check-off progress, and preferences are stored locally on your device (including the App Group container used by the widget). We do not operate any servers, and we never collect, transmit, or have access to your data. PackNest does not require an account.

Fully offline

Creating trips, checking items, browsing templates, and exporting a PDF all happen on your device. PackNest never sends anything to our servers (we do not run any).

System APIs

PackNest uses UserDefaults and local file timestamps to save settings and mirror a widget snapshot (declared under Required Reason APIs in the privacy manifest). That data still stays on your device and is not uploaded to us.

No ads, no tracking

PackNest contains no third-party advertising, no analytics SDKs, and no tracking of any kind. This is a permanent product policy.

In-app purchases

PackNest Pro is a one-time non-consumable purchase processed entirely by Apple. We do not receive or store any payment information. Entitlement is verified on-device by StoreKit.

Your rights (GDPR / CCPA)

Because your data lives only on your device: you can access and correct it in the app, and deleting your data means clearing trips in the app or deleting the app itself. We cannot access your data, and we do not sell any personal information (there is none we could sell). For privacy inquiries, contact the email below.

Children's privacy

PackNest does not collect personal information from anyone, including children. There are no accounts and no social features.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.
