About

I'm the solo developer behind Sparkfall Games. Every game and tool is 100% native to iOS, localized in 34 languages, and built around one rule: your data stays on your device.

I design, code, draw and compose everything myself, so each app stays small, fast and honest. No investors and no growth hacks — when you buy one of my apps, you're the customer, not the product.
