SevenSins（七罪歌剧院）– 隐私政策

最后更新 / Last updated: 2026-08-14

你的数据只存在你的设备上

SevenSins 是一款完全单机的歌剧院经营游戏。你的游戏进度、剧情选择、收藏与偏好设置都只保存在你的设备本地。我们不运营任何服务器，不收集、不传输、也无法访问你的任何数据。游戏不需要注册账号。

完全离线

全部玩法、剧情与美术内容都内置在 App 里，游戏本身不含任何联网代码。唯一的网络活动来自 Apple 自身的 StoreKit（购买 / 恢复内购时），由 Apple 处理。

系统能力说明

SevenSins 使用 UserDefaults 与本地文件时间戳等系统 API 保存设置与游戏存档（见 App 隐私清单中的 Required Reason API 声明）。这些数据仍留在设备上，不会上传给我们。

无广告、无追踪

SevenSins 不含任何第三方广告、统计分析 SDK 或任何形式的追踪。这是永久产品政策。

内购说明

内购为一次性买断（如 All-Access 通行证与可选的服装外观），由 Apple 全程处理交易，我们不接收、不存储任何支付信息。购买状态由设备上的 StoreKit 校验。本游戏没有抽卡、开箱或任何「花钱买随机结果」的机制，也没有订阅。

你的权利（GDPR / CCPA）

由于数据只存在于你的设备上：访问与更正可直接在游戏内完成；删除数据 = 在游戏内重置存档或直接删除 App。我们无法访问你的任何数据，因此也不存在可出售的个人信息——我们不出售任何个人信息。隐私相关问询请联系下方邮箱。

成人分级与儿童隐私

SevenSins 在 App Store 的年龄分级为 18+，面向成年玩家，不面向儿童。游戏不收集任何人（包括儿童）的个人信息，没有账号，也没有聊天或社交功能——游戏内的「对话」均为与虚构角色的单机剧情内容。

政策变更

本政策如有更新会发布在本页面，并刷新顶部日期。

SevenSins – Privacy Policy (English)

Your data stays on your device

SevenSins is a fully offline, single-player opera-house management game. Your game progress, story choices, collections, and preferences are stored locally on your device. We do not operate any servers, and we never collect, transmit, or have access to your data. The game does not require an account.

Fully offline

All gameplay, story, and art content ships inside the app; the game itself contains no networking code. The only network activity comes from Apple's own StoreKit (when buying or restoring purchases), which is handled by Apple.

System APIs

SevenSins uses UserDefaults and local file timestamps to save settings and game saves (declared under Required Reason APIs in the privacy manifest). That data stays on your device and is never uploaded to us.

No ads, no tracking

SevenSins contains no third-party advertising, no analytics SDKs, and no tracking of any kind. This is a permanent product policy.

In-app purchases

Purchases are one-time unlocks (such as the All-Access pass and optional outfit cosmetics), processed entirely by Apple. We never receive or store any payment information. Purchase state is verified on-device via StoreKit. There are no loot boxes, no gacha, no paid randomness of any kind, and no subscriptions.

Your rights (GDPR / CCPA)

Because your data exists only on your device: access and correction happen directly in the game; deleting your data means resetting the save in-game or deleting the app. We cannot access any of your data, so there is no personal information to sell — and we do not sell any personal information. For privacy inquiries, contact the email below.

Adult rating & children's privacy

SevenSins is rated 18+ on the App Store and is intended for adult players; it is not directed at children. The game collects no personal information from anyone, including children. There are no accounts and no chat or social features — all in-game "conversations" are single-player story content with fictional characters.

Changes to this policy
