FAQ (English)

What is free and what does All-Access include?

The free tier includes the full opera-house management core and two divas' story lines (with the first levels of bond progression). All-Access is a one-time purchase (not a subscription): it unlocks all divas, complete story lines, and full progression. Optional outfit cosmetics may be sold later, also as one-time purchases. There is no gacha, ever — everything has a fixed, visible price and there is no paid randomness.

How do I restore my purchase?

Open Settings or the purchase page in the game and tap “Restore Purchases”, using the same Apple ID as the original purchase.

Where is my save? What about a new phone?

Your save lives only on your device. Deleting the app permanently deletes the save. When switching devices, migrate via a full iOS device backup (iCloud or computer backup).

Do I need a network connection?

No. The game is fully playable offline; only buying or restoring purchases uses Apple's StoreKit.

What age is this game for?

SevenSins is rated 18+ on the App Store and contains mature and suggestive (non-explicit) content intended for adult players.

Am I chatting with real people?

No. All conversations are single-player story content with fictional characters. There is no online chat, social feature, or user interaction of any kind.

Do I need a subscription?

No. All-Access is a single one-time purchase. No subscription, no gacha, no loot boxes — ever.

Does it work offline?

Yes. The whole opera house runs on-device, offline. Your save never leaves your iPhone.

常见问题

哪些内容免费？All-Access 有什么？

免费层可完整游玩歌剧院经营主线与两位名伶的攻略线（含心弦培养前几级）。All-Access 是一次买断（不是订阅）：解锁全部名伶、完整剧情线与培养上限。后续可能推出可选的服装外观单卖，同样为一次性购买。本游戏永不抽卡——所有内容明码标价，没有任何付费随机。

如何恢复购买？

打开游戏内「设置」或购买页，点「恢复购买」，使用当初购买时的同一 Apple 账户即可。

我的存档在哪里？换手机怎么办？

存档只保存在你的设备本地。删除 App 会永久删除存档，请谨慎操作。更换设备时可通过 iOS 系统的整机备份迁移（iCloud 备份或电脑备份均可）。

需要联网吗？

不需要。游戏完全离线可玩，只有购买 / 恢复内购时由 Apple 的 StoreKit 联网处理。

这款游戏适合什么年龄？

SevenSins 的 App Store 分级为 18+，含成熟主题与暧昧内容（非露骨），面向成年玩家。

游戏里的「对话」是和真人聊天吗？

不是。全部对话都是单机剧情内容，对象是虚构角色，没有任何联网聊天、社交或用户互动功能。
